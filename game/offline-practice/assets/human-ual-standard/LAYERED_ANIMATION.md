# Layered derivative

HumanBasketballLayered.glb is a local derivative of the accompanying CC0
Quaternius Universal Animation Library Standard mannequin and the locally
authored HumanBasketball.glb. License.txt, README.txt and AUTHORED_ANIMATION.md
retain the original and authored-animation provenance.

tool/author-basketball-layered.cjs adds property masks: five body clips retain
191 channels each; six arm clips contain four upper/lower-arm rotation channels.
No rig, geometry, material, accessor or binary animation data is changed.
The original clips and both baseline GLB files remain intact.

These masks support independent sampling in the app. They are not a basketball
animation supplied by Flutter Scene and do not establish runtime compatibility
or completed jump/shoot animation without renderer testing.
